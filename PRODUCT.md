# Joseph Lteif’s portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Hiring teams, recruiters, and technical leaders assessing Joseph’s engineering strengths and fit, then finding his resume or contacting him.

## Product Purpose
Present Joseph’s work through a guided, personal story: introduction, selected projects, career results, Lebanese Red Cross service, working notes, and contact.

## Positioning
A software engineer building practical products and playful experiences across web, desktop, and mobile. The portfolio connects real engineering decisions and outcomes to the person behind them.

## Operating Context
Visitors review selected projects, professional experience, technologies used in context, service, the downloadable resume, and external project or profile links.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Existing React 19, Vite, vanilla CSS, and Lucide setup; GitHub Pages deployment.
- Preserve light/dark themes, responsive navigation, resume access, contact details, and project links.
- Content and sections may be rewritten, reordered, or condensed while retaining factual accuracy.
- Replace the interactive terminal with concise, readable working notes; weave skills into work and experience.
- No new dependencies, APIs, data schemas, or deployment changes are required.

## Brand Commitments
Joseph Lteif’s real identity, existing portrait, project assets, and body of work. Practical and playful, personal and approachable. Corporate stiffness is an explicitly unwanted outcome. The user prefers safer, familiar designs. The portrait should feel integrated through shape and spacing, without blur or a straight-edged frame.

## Evidence on Hand
- `public/profile.jpg`: existing portrait of Joseph.
- `public/project-logos/`: existing project marks.
- `public/Joseph_Lteif_Resume_V27.pdf`: current resume; `archive/Joseph_Lteif_Resume_V26.pdf` preserves the previous version.
- `src/portfolioData.js`: Nightfall, WoW Simulation Tool, Pocket Ledger (in development), and Invoice Generator, with descriptions, features, technologies, and links.
- `src/components/Experience.jsx`: existing career history and reported engineering outcomes.
- `src/components/Volunteering.jsx`: Lebanese Red Cross service since March 2018, leadership, training, and crisis response.
- Existing content is the factual source. Do not fabricate anecdotes, testimonials, employers, users, or results.

## Product Principles
- Make real work and engineering decisions easy to understand.
- Let practical utility and playful curiosity coexist.
- Ground personal warmth in real service and experience.
- Make resume and contact access immediate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Support mobile and desktop, semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, and readable light/dark themes.
